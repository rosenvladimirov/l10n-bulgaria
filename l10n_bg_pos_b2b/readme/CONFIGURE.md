In *Point of Sale > Configuration > Settings*, section **Wholesale (B2B)**:

* **Wholesale Mode**: Off, Wholesale button, or Automatic for business
  customers. Off removes the wholesale block; business customers are still
  always invoiced. Wholesale mode needs an invoice journal.
* **Invoice Data Check**: Warn (the cashier confirms, the confirmation is
  noted on the order) or Block.
* **Credit Limit**: policy Warn (default) or Block, and the maximum amount
  on account per order while offline (0 = no cap). The credit limit is the
  one of the customer (Accounting setting "Sales Credit Limit").

Give the group **Wholesale in Point of Sale** to the cashiers who sell
wholesale. Point of Sale administrators have it already.
