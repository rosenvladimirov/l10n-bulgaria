Wholesale (B2B) sales in the Point of Sale saved as sale orders.

In the Wholesale mode of the Point of Sale (module `l10n_bg_pos_b2b`) the
cashier saves the order as a quotation: a draft sale order is created (or
updated) with the customer, invoice and delivery addresses, pricelist,
payment terms, expiration, delivery date, salesperson, warehouse, customer
reference, note and the order lines with their prices and discounts. The
sale order number is shown in the order details. The quotation and the
pro-forma invoice are printed inside the Point of Sale, over the products,
and closed with one button.

Wholesale sales need a connection: the Wholesale button is locked while the
Point of Sale is offline.
