Bulgarian Accounting Reports

Technical module for generating reports and declarations for the Bulgarian National Revenue Agency (NAP)

This is a technical module designed to build the necessary reports and files for declaration to the National Revenue Agency (NAP). It is not used independently, but in conjunction with EE or CE modules that generate the corresponding print forms and declaration files.

Key Features

VAT Reports

VAT Report-Declaration, Sales and Purchase reports compliant with Bulgarian standards

VIES Reports

EU VAT Information Exchange System reporting for Bulgarian companies

Account Management

Advanced account tags management and company configuration extensions

Configuration Process

Step 1

Go to Accounting -> Configuration -> Settings

Step 2

Set up Bulgarian-specific options

Step 3

Configure company information
