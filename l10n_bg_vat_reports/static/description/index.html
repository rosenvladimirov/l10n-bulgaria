Bulgarian Accounting Reports (EE)

Specialized module for generating accounting reports and declarations in compliance with Bulgarian legislation

Version 19.0.3.0.3 - This module provides automated generation and validation of reports required by Bulgarian National Revenue Agency (NRA). It is designed to work in conjunction with other EE modules.

Key Features

VAT Reports

VAT Declaration (Art. 125), Sales and Purchase Ledgers (Art. 124) compliant with Bulgarian VAT Act

EC Sales List

VIES Declaration under Art. 125 of the Bulgarian VAT Act for EU transactions

NRA Integration

Automated CSV generation, built-in validation, and export capabilities
